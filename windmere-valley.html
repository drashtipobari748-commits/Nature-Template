    Questions

    Can I use this for something other than nature?

    Yes. The landscape is only the backdrop. Change the words and sections for any project.

    How do I change the colors?

    Each season is one entry in the S object in the script. Edit its hex values and the scene updates.

    Do the effects stay in the hero?

    Yes. Weather, snow and lightning only appear in the landscape at the top.

    Can I remove the season and weather panel?

    Delete the element with id ctl. The page then stays in summer with clear weather.
